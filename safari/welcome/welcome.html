Welcome to Dynamic RTL

Persian and Arabic pages, automatically right-to-left with a beautiful font.

  1. 1Pin the icon: open the extensions menu (puzzle piece) and pin Dynamic RTL to the toolbar.
  2. 2Toggle any site: click the icon on a page to turn RTL on or off just for that site.
  3. 3Fine-tune: open Settings for the site list, custom fonts and the keyboard shortcut.

Tip: press Ctrl+Shift+Y (Command+Shift+Y on Mac) on any page to toggle it instantly.
